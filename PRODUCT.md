# Product

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Dranx Prediction League is a private friends' competition for the 2026/27 Premier League. Participants commit one season prediction, compare their table and seven Spotlight picks, and play a separate weekly Win Streak game. The owner reviews and publishes results.

## Capabilities and constraints

The existing Next.js application owns all data and interactions. Preserve the three-stage entry, browser draft, immutable submission, receipt privacy, database-enforced cutoffs, shared competition ranks, and separate table and Spotlight scoring. Win Streak supports same-name receipt recovery without accounts. No new personal information or customization workflow is required.

## Brand commitments

Preserve the purple identity and original local club badges. The owner approved the September 5 redesign reference, with a compact podium retained. Every participant tied at a podium rank receives equal visual status.

## Evidence on hand

The approved interactive HTML and text companion are in the owner's September 5 visualization directory. Repository source and authenticated, bounded production reads establish real product state. Prototype data is illustrative and must not enter production.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Support keyboard, touch, screen readers, reduced motion, light and dark themes, and 320–430px mobile layouts. Names and numerical ranks carry identity and meaning independently of decorative animal icons or color.
