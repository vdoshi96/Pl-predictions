# Project

Dranx Prediction League is an unofficial prediction game for a private invited group covering the 2026/27 English Premier League. A participant completes one immutable three-stage entry: order exactly 20 clubs and enter one display name, choose all seven spotlight predictions, then review the whole package before submission. The categories are top scorer, top assister, most clean sheets, underdog team, overrated team, underdog player, and overrated player. The first, second, sixth, and seventh choose a searchable player or Other-player name; the other three choose one of the existing clubs. The initial entry response contains no player catalogue or portrait paths. Stage 2 lazily loads active current-season players through a same-origin route; player search requires two normalized characters, shows at most 20 matches while announcing the total, and always retains Other player.

Before reveal, the public table leaderboard exposes only each participant's name, submission time, predicted champion, and 0-point total. Prediction IDs, positions 2–20, and all seven spotlight picks stay private to the receipt browser and administrator. After reveal, the main leaderboard compares each table with the active standings. Its 5–3–1 tiers have a maximum of 100. A separate spotlight page tracks fun accuracy and never changes table points. Let `N` be the current number of active, nondeleted season brackets. Accuracy points are `max(0, N + 1 - outcome rank)`. Overall accuracy sums only resolved categories. Pending categories remain unavailable. Equal overall scores share a competition rank.

Team underdog index is average predicted position minus actual position. Team overrated index is the inverse, and the largest value ranks first. Top scorer, top assister, and most clean sheets use the selected subject's occupied result rank. Underdog player ranks reviewed season ratings from high to low; overrated player uses the same factual ratings from low to high. The owner enters those five factual views manually through `/admin/results`, backed by sealed immutable `goals`, `assists`, `clean_sheets`, and shared `player_ratings` snapshots with exact pinned Other-name aliases. The app has no runtime football-data access. The owner-selected 2026-08-13 selector snapshot supplies 582 players and 582 local portraits; it is internally reconciled and not independently official-site verified. The server SQL-filters active catalogue rows, and submission validation queries only player IDs referenced by the submitted picks. Other player remains available. The refreshed production catalogue has 599 total player rows: 582 active with 582 portrait paths and 17 preserved inactive rows.

Primary users are invited participants on mobile devices and one owner-administrator. The browser retains a validated season-local draft until submission, and untouched A–Z tables require explicit intent. Arsenal v Coventry City's verified `2026-08-21T19:00:00.000Z` opening kickoff is the persisted, sole timed submission cutoff for the 2026/27 season. There is no configurable earlier deadline. Settings always show the fixed instant in `America/Chicago` plus a selectable Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, or UTC rendering with DST-aware labels. The owner may close earlier only through irreversible typed manual lock or early reveal, requiring exact `LOCK` or `REVEAL`; both reveal predictions and permanently close submissions. The owner can also delete erroneous entries, manage standings/import visibility, enter and version factual result datasets, and confirm or undo exact final pointers. Login uses the `admin` username by default with a server-only salted PBKDF2 password hash; `ADMIN_SECRET` is a migration fallback only. Deleting a prediction cascades through its 20 table items and seven spotlight picks, after which the same normalized name may submit again and consensus scores derive from the remaining entries.

The user-facing visual system uses the Premier League's official purple, `#37003c`, with cyan `#05f0ff`, green `#00ff87`, and pink `#ff2882` accents and an original Dranx mark. The official Premier League logo is not included. The project owner supplied and directed use of 20 transparent local PNG club badges; `TeamMark` displays them with contain sizing and an accessible initials fallback, while the original monograms remain rollback-only for the first PNG release.

Constraints: one Next.js/Vercel application, Vercel-managed Neon, and one atomic submission with 20 table rows and seven category rows. The app has no end-user accounts, runtime football API, production scraper, cron, or extra personal data. It remains compatible with free and Hobby tiers. Operational identifiers retain their existing `pl-predictions` names. `/rules` explains table points, separate spotlight accuracy, and pending outcomes.
