Concept pitch Not built yet 2026/27 season

Keep the group coming back after the one big entry.

Dranx already has the season-long game: one immutable 20-club table and seven spotlight picks, scored from imported standings and the manual results desk. These nine mini-games sit beside that. They never change the 100-point table board or spotlight accuracy. They reuse display names, club badges, the player catalogue, and owner paste desks. No live football API, no cron, no extra accounts.

House rules every idea obeys

The season entry stays sacred. Mini-game points live on their own boards. They never edit table points, never change spotlight accuracy, and never rewrite a submitted prediction.
Same friends, same names. Only people who already submitted a display name can play. The existing receipt cookie identifies them. No new accounts, emails, or personal data.
Owner paste, not a live feed. Fixtures, weekend results, monthly deltas, and transfer verdicts enter through authenticated admin desks, the same way standings and spotlight facts already do.
Mobile first, two-minute taps. A weekly game should be finishable on a phone in under two minutes, with 56px targets and no horizontal overflow. Instant quizzes can be shorter.
Locks are explicit and honest. A round either locks at an owner-set kickoff instant or stays open. A late or already-closed save is a truthful no-op, same spirit as season lock.
Hobby-tier cheap. No scrapers, no Vercel Cron, no image hotlinks. Local badges and the 578 portraits already on the site are the visual kit.

01Weekend Slip

Weekly Owner paste First to ship

The Friday pint game. The owner posts six to eight featured fixtures. Everyone taps Win / Draw / Away before the first of those kickoffs. Results land through a paste desk on Sunday or Monday. A separate Slip board ranks the group. Season table points do not move.

How it behaves

  1. Thursday or Friday, the owner pastes a reviewed fixture list for the gameweek: home, away, and a lock instant.
  2. /play/slip opens for submitted names only. Each fixture shows two club badges and three fat buttons: Home, Draw, Away.
  3. A participant can change picks until the lock instant. After lock, the slip is read-only. A late submit is a no-op.
  4. After the weekend, the owner pastes the six-to-eight results. The board updates from the sealed weekend snapshot, never from a live feed.
  5. The page shows your week score, the group table, and a “you vs the modal pick” row so the chat has something to argue about.

Rules

  1. One slip per display name per gameweek.
  2. Every posted fixture must be filled. Partial slips do not lock in.
  3. Correct result (1X2) scores 1 point. No exact-score line. Keep it fast.
  4. A postponed match is void and ignored. Remaining fixtures still score.
  5. Missed lock = zero for that week, not a penalty on the season board.
  6. Season Slip total is the sum of week scores. Ties share rank.

Why 1X2 and not exact scores: friends actually finish a six-fixture slip on the train. Exact scores become homework and empty slips.

02Last Club Standing

Weekly Elimination First to ship

Classic survivor, Premier League flavoured. Each gameweek you pick one club you think will win its match. You cannot reuse a club. Pick a loser or a draw and you are out. Last name standing gets a silly crown on the site until May.

How it behaves

  1. The owner posts the gameweek’s 10 pairings, or a shorter featured set if a midweek slate is messy. Each club appears at most once.
  2. Alive players see a 20-club grid. Used clubs are struck through. Eliminated players see a tombstone and the remaining field.
  3. Lock is the earliest kickoff of any club still available that week. After lock, picks reveal to the group so the group chat can groan.
  4. Owner pastes the week’s winning clubs. Draws and losses eliminate anyone who picked that club. Unused picks on an eliminated player stay unused.
  5. If the whole remaining field dies on the same week, they all re-enter that week with their used-club lists intact. Nobody lucks into a bye.

Rules

  1. One living pick per week. No doubles, no player reuse.
  2. Win only. Draw or defeat eliminates you.
  3. A postponed club is unselectable that week if the owner marks it void.
  4. Missed lock while alive = elimination. The grid is the reminder.
  5. Optional Christmas chip: each alive player may, once, reuse a previously used club between GW17 and GW21. Makes the festive congestion playable.
  6. If two or more are still alive at the final gameweek and they all survive it, they share the crown. No sudden-death extra data.

03The Halfway Rewrite

One shot Reuses table UI ~GW19

The most Dranx mini-game that is not a new sport. Around the halfway mark, everyone may submit a second 20-club final table: “knowing what I know now, this is how it finishes.” The original August entry stays frozen and keeps scoring the main 100-point board. The rewrite gets its own podium.

How it behaves

  1. Owner opens the window after a chosen standings import — typically the table after GW19 — and sets a lock at the next Saturday kickoff.
  2. /play/rewrite loads the existing Stage 1 reorderer, prefilled with the live table so nobody starts from A–Z by accident. An untouched live-table order still needs the same explicit acknowledgement pattern the season form already uses.
  3. No spotlight picks. This is table-only, five minutes on a phone.
  4. After lock, rewrites reveal next to original champions so the group can see who blinked. Scoring waits for the final standings snapshot.

Rules

  1. One rewrite per display name. Same atomic write: all 20 clubs or nothing.
  2. Scoring copies the main table curve: 5 for exact place, 3 within three, 1 for correct half, cap 100. Separate board.
  3. A player who skips it simply does not appear on the rewrite board.
  4. The rewrite cannot be used to edit or replace the August entry.
Owner work Open/close the window. Final table already exists at season end.
Reuses Drag-and-drop table, A–Z acknowledgement, 5–3–1 scorer, badges.
Come-back hook A second chance without rewriting history. Perfect January conversation.

04Face in the Crowd

Anytime Zero match data First to ship

You already host 578 local portraits and a searchable 580-player catalogue. During international breaks — when Weekend Slip has nothing to lock — the site becomes a pub-quiz machine. Each day in a break week, one seeded puzzle: a cropped or silhouetted face and four names.

How it behaves

  1. A daily puzzle is derived on the server from the active catalogue and a season-day seed. No owner fixture paste. Other-player names never appear as the answer.
  2. The portrait starts as a silhouette or an eyes-only crop. After you answer, the real PlayerMark face reveals, with the silhouette fallback for the two known missing portraits.
  3. Four names: the answer plus three distractors from other clubs. One tap. Immediate right/wrong. You cannot change it.
  4. A break-week board sums correct answers. Ties break on faster first tap, recorded server-side when the answer lands.

Rules

  1. One answer per display name per puzzle. First tap is final.
  2. Correct = 1. Wrong or skip = 0.
  3. Ryan McAidoo and Luc De Fougerolles are excluded as answers.
  4. The initial homepage still must not ship the catalogue. The quiz route lazy-loads faces the same way Stage 2 already does.
  5. This is entertainment. It never feeds result rankings.

05Contrarian Chip

One chip Uses consensus After reveal

The group’s average table is already a character in Dranx. This game lets you bet against it. After reveal, each person places one chip on one club: “they finish higher than we think” or “they finish lower than we think.” The existing underdog / overrated index does the maths.

How it behaves

  1. Once season predictions are revealed, /play/contrarian lists the 20 clubs with the live consensus average and current actual position.
  2. You pick one club and one direction: Upstart or Overhyped. Lock is owner-set, defaulting to the next Saturday after reveal.
  3. After every standings import the chip shows a live index, using the same formulas as spotlight team categories: upstart = average predicted position − actual, overhyped = the inverse.
  4. At the final table, the largest index in the direction you chose wins. Negative index is a proud zero, not a debt.

Rules

  1. One chip, one club, one direction. No swaps after lock.
  2. You may chip a club you already picked as champion or as a spotlight team.
  3. Scoring is the raw index, full precision, then ranked. Ties share.
  4. A chip placed after lock is rejected. No silent overwrite.
  5. This board is independent of the underdog / overrated spotlight scores.
Owner work Open the window after reveal. Standings imports already refresh the index.
Reuses Consensus averages, expectation-index helpers, imported table, badges.
Come-back hook Every new table answers “was I right to fade the group?”

06Monthly Armband

Monthly Uses result snapshots

Each calendar month you captain one player. They score from the delta between two published result snapshots you already know how to seal: goals plus assists for that player in that month. No new stat type. No weekly match-by-match feed.

How it behaves

  1. On the 1st, or when the owner opens the month, the Stage 2 player selector appears. Other player stays available.
  2. Lock is 12:00 America/Chicago on the 2nd, or the first league kickoff of the month if that is earlier. Same dual-zone display as settings.
  3. During the month the armband page is just a gallery of captains and portraits. No live numbers until the owner publishes.
  4. Owner publishes the usual goals and assists datasets. The month score for a player is (goals_now + assists_now) − (goals_prev + assists_prev) using the last sealed snapshots from the previous month. First month uses a zero baseline.

Rules

  1. One captain per display name per month.
  2. You may repeat a captain in a later month.
  3. Month points = the nonnegative delta. A player who scores nothing is a valid zero.
  4. Other-player captains only score after the owner pins the exact alias on that month’s snapshot, same as spotlight.
  5. Unresolved or unpublished month stays pending. No invented stats.
  6. Season Armband total sums resolved months only. Ties share rank.
Owner work Keep doing monthly result publishes. The delta is derived.
Reuses Player selector, Other-player aliases, sealed goals / assists snapshots, portraits.
Come-back hook A new argument every 1st of the month without a new data pipeline.

07Hot Take Tribunal

On demand Social

The owner posts one sentence. The group votes Yes or No before a lock. Some takes resolve from facts you already store. Some resolve when the owner bangs the gavel. The page is a timeline of takes, vote splits, and who looks clever.

How it behaves

  1. Owner writes a take, a lock instant, and a resolve mode: gavel, final table place, or spotlight fact.
  2. Friends see the sentence, two giant buttons, and a live count only after they vote. No preview of the split first — stops pile-ons.
  3. After lock, the split reveals. Resolve happens later: either a typed YES / NO gavel, or a derived check such as “Arsenal finish 1st” against the final standings.

Rules

  1. One vote per take. Changeable until lock, then frozen.
  2. Correct side scores 1. Wrong scores 0. Unresolved stays pending.
  3. Derived takes may only read imported standings or published result snapshots. They may not invent a live table.
  4. Gavel takes require the exact typed phrase and are irreversible, same caution as season LOCK / REVEAL.
  5. A take with no votes at lock is void, not a forced result.

Example takes that already fit the data: “Sunderland finish top half.” “Haaland is the published top scorer.” “The group’s average champion is wrong.” Gavel takes cover everything spicier: “this manager is gone by March.”

Owner work Write the sentence. Gavel or wait for a snapshot you were publishing anyway.
Reuses Display names, standings, result snapshots, irreversible-phrase UX.
Come-back hook Screenshot bait. The group chat already talks like this — put it on the site.

08Festive Frenzy

Holiday window Uses Weekend Slip

A named mini-league over the Boxing Day to New Year congestion. It is not a new engine. It is Weekend Slip with a gold frame, a shorter calendar, and a trophy that is allowed to be tasteless.

How it behaves

  1. Owner marks a contiguous run of posted slates as the Festive window — typically the fixtures from 26 December through 3 January.
  2. The same 1X2 slip UI is used. A festive skin and a countdown sit on /play/slip.
  3. A dedicated Frenzy board sums only those slates. The main Slip season board still counts them too, so nobody is punished for playing Christmas.
  4. Optional bauble: during the window, Last Club Standing’s one reuse chip becomes available if that game is live.

Rules

  1. Same 1X2 scoring as Weekend Slip.
  2. Only owner-tagged festive slates count on the Frenzy board.
  3. Ties share the bauble. No extra tie-break match is invented.
  4. If a festive slate is voided, Frenzy simply has one fewer round.
Owner work Tag the window. No new result format.
Reuses Weekend Slip snapshots, badges, display names.
Come-back hook A holiday title people will mention in May. Cheap once Slip exists.

09January Window

1–31 January Owner gavel

Transfer deadline day is the only mid-season event the whole group already watches. Each person files three sealed rumours: a club, a direction (in or out), and a player from the catalogue or Other player. After the window, the owner gavels each rumour against completed deals.

How it behaves

  1. Market opens 1 January 00:00 America/Chicago and locks 31 January at an owner-set hour before the real deadline, so nobody is still typing during the last TV crawl.
  2. Three slots. Each slot: club badge picker, In or Out, then the same player selector Stage 2 already uses. Out rumours should name a player currently at that club; the server checks the active catalogue. In rumours may be Other player.
  3. Slips stay private until lock, then they publish as a rumour wall.
  4. After deadline day the owner marks each rumour landed, close, or miss. Close is for the right club and direction but the wrong player.

Rules

  1. Exactly three rumours, or the file is rejected.
  2. Landed = 3, close = 1, miss = 0.
  3. Loans count if the owner says they count on that rumour’s gavel.
  4. Unfinished or collapsed deals are misses, not pending forever.
  5. The catalogue is still a selector, not an outcome feed. A completed deal is an owner fact, same as a result snapshot.
  6. Window points never touch table or spotlight scores.
Owner work One gavel pass after 1 February. Highest drama per minute of admin.
Reuses Clubs, player selector, Other player, irreversible admin phrases.
Come-back hook Deadline-night energy in a group that already lives in the rumour mill.

What I would ship, and in what order

Wave 1

The weekly habit

  1. Weekend Slip
  2. Last Club Standing, reading the same result snapshot
  3. Face in the Crowd for empty Saturdays

This is the engagement engine. One new admin desk (fixtures + 1X2 results) powers two games. The quiz needs no desk.

Wave 2

The Dranx-flavoured pair

  1. Contrarian Chip
  2. The Halfway Rewrite
  3. Hot Take Tribunal

These talk to data you already compute: consensus, the table reorderer, imported standings. Low new surface, high in-jokes.

Wave 3

The calendar events

  1. Monthly Armband
  2. Festive Frenzy (skin on Slip)
  3. January Window

Ship these when the year actually reaches them. Frenzy is almost free after Wave 1. The window is a one-month product.

Ideas I would not add

  • Exact-score jackpots or real-money stakes. This is a friends league with display names only. Keep it a pint, not a betting product.
  • A live match centre or scrapers. The app’s boundary is no runtime football API, no production scraper, no cron. Mini-games that need 3pm live scores would break that on purpose.
  • Editing the August table mid-season. The immutability is the sport. The Halfway Rewrite exists so people can be wiser without pretending they always were.
  • Fantasy XI with weekly player points. That is a second product and a weekly stats feed. Monthly Armband is the honest version of the same itch.
  • Pushing mini-game points into the 100-point table. Spotlight accuracy is already the fun side-board. More side-boards are fine. Mixing them is how the main race stops meaning anything.

Shared technical shape, if any of this is built

  • New routes under /play/…, linked from the post-reveal home and the nav, never injected into the pre-reveal prediction form.
  • Each mini-game write is atomic for that game’s row set, with the same signed admin session and same-origin checks on owner mutations.
  • Weekend results and festive tags are sealed snapshots with last-good preservation, copied from standings/results discipline.
  • Public HTML still must not leak unrevealed season picks. Mini-game picks can be public after their own lock, not before.
  • Scoring stays derived. No editable totals. Pending beats invented zero.
